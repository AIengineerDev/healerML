## How to sign

Open a pull request. A bot will comment asking you to sign. Reply with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your GitHub username and the timestamp are recorded in
`signatures/v1/cla.json`. You sign once, for all future contributions.

**Contributing on behalf of a company?** Individual signature may not be valid
if your employer owns your work product. Open an issue and we will arrange a
Corporate CLA.
